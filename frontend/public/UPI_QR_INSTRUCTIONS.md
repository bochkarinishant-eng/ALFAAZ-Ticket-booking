# Where to add your actual UPI QR code image

You can replace the placeholder UPI QR image anytime with your actual Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM UPI QR screenshot!

### Image Location:
Replace this file:
`frontend/public/upi-qr.png`
(or `frontend/public/upi-qr.svg`)

### Recommended specs:
- **Format:** PNG, JPG, or SVG
- **Resolution:** 500x500 pixels or higher, square aspect ratio
- **Size:** Under 1 MB
- **Filename:** `upi-qr.png`
